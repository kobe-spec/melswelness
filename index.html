About

Meet Melanie

My career in wellness spans more than 20 years of massage therapy, Reiki, reflexology and holistic health. I have worked in medical, chiropractic and spa settings, and as a massage therapy instructor. I specialize in plantar fasciitis, sciatica, lymphatic drainage and frozen shoulder.

I believe massage does not have to hurt to be effective. Focusing on your sense of comfort, I incorporate multiple techniques with a firm touch to melt tissue layer by layer, alleviating pain and bringing relief to chronic discomfort rather than using excessive pressure — which is often counterproductive.

My goal is to assess the individual needs of each client and provide a tailored session, whether that is simple relaxation and stress reduction or focused clinical treatment. With massage as a key element of the process, plus education on self-care at home, I can help you reach pain management, increased range of motion and general well-being.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance?

It helps, but same-week appointments are often available — and last-minute requests are worth a call. Visitors staying on the Grand Strand are regularly fitted in.

Does massage have to hurt to be effective?

No. That is a myth, and often counterproductive. Mel works with a firm touch that melts tissue layer by layer rather than forcing through it.

What should I expect on a first visit?

A short consultation about what is going on, then a session built around it. You leave with suggestions for self-care you can do at home between appointments.

Do you work with post-surgical clients?

Yes. Post-operative lymphatic care is one of the studio’s specialties, with 19 years focused on lymphatic work specifically.

Can you come to my house or rental?

Yes — in-home massage is available across the Grand Strand with a two-person minimum. Table, linens and hot towels all come with us.

What conditions do you specialize in?

Plantar fasciitis, sciatica, frozen shoulder and lymphatic drainage, alongside general wellness and relaxation work.
